  en: |
    - Built a six-section site on Next.js 16 and React 19: words of the day, AI conversation, speaking, reading, games and progress. There are no accounts and no database: four API routes only call the AI and store nothing, and progress lives in the browser's localStorage.
    - The daily set is generated by AI: 5 words with a Russian translation, a definition and an example, 3 Russian sentences to translate, a mini text and an open question. A zod schema checks the model's output, the set is cached in the browser for the whole day, and its words feed the Word Match game. Learners compare their own translation with a model answer, and nothing is graded.
    - AI conversation: free chat or one of 7 role-play scenarios (airport, job interview, restaurant, hotel, shop, negotiation, chatting with a friend) at three difficulty levels. The system prompt allows at most one gentle correction per reply, with no grammar lectures and no scores. Speaking mode runs the same dialogue by voice through the Web Speech API: the browser recognises speech and reads the AI's reply aloud.
    - Reading: a 120–200-word text on one of 6 topics with 3 comprehension questions and model answers for self-checking. Games: Word Match, pairing words with translations against the clock, and chess on chess.js against a bot that plays random legal moves, while an AI coach comments on every move the player makes and slips in chess and English phrases.
    - Answers are never scored: XP and coins come for completed activities, with levels, day streaks, 8 badges and a shop on top. The no-grading rule is built into the data types themselves — there is no score field for an answer.
    - Connected the AI through an adapter with a single interface: Gemini, which has a free tier, by default and OpenAI as an alternative, switched with one environment variable. Deployed the site on Vercel.
